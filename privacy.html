Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

This policy explains how SalemDomain MobileTech handles information in connection with this website (salemdomainmobiletech.rf.gd) and, at a general level, the desktop platform it describes.

This website

This website does not include a contact form, account sign-up, or tracking scripts. It does not use cookies or third-party analytics. If you contact us by email or WhatsApp, we receive whatever information you choose to include in that message, such as your name, email address, and phone number.

Like most web servers, the hosting provider for this site may log standard technical information (such as IP address and browser type) for security and performance purposes. We do not use this information for tracking or advertising.

The desktop platform

The SalemDomain MobileTech desktop platform is a separate application used by technicians to run diagnostics and services on customer devices. It handles account information (such as login credentials and billing/credit balance) and device identifiers (such as serial numbers and ECIDs) needed to provide and bill for services. This website does not itself collect that information; it is collected within the platform when you use it.

How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries sent by email or WhatsApp.
  • To operate and bill for services within the desktop platform.
  • To maintain the security and reliability of our systems.

Third-party services

Billing within the platform is processed through PesaPal. When you make a payment, PesaPal handles the payment details directly; we do not store your card or mobile money credentials.

Data retention

We retain account and service records for as long as needed to provide the service and meet our accounting obligations, and delete or anonymize information we no longer need for these purposes.

Your choices

You can contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you, request a correction, or request deletion where we are not required to retain it for legal or accounting reasons.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes to the website or platform. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your information can be sent to info@salemdomain.com.
